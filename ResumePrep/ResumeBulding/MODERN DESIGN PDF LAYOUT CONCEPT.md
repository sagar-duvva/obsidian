# **MODERN DESIGN PDF LAYOUT CONCEPT**

**Structure:**

1. **Header** (Name + Contact Info, subtle accent color, professional font)
    
2. **Professional Summary** (1–2 sentences, bold keywords)
    
3. **Core Skills / Competencies** (Icons optional for cloud, automation, security)
    
4. **Experience Section** (Company | Role | Dates aligned right; achievements in bullet points with strong action verbs)
    
5. **Education & Certifications** (bottom-left)
    
6. **Keywords / Tools** (bottom-right, small font for ATS visibility)
    
7. **Design Tips:**
    
    - Use 1.0–1.1 line spacing for readability.
        
    - Keep 0.5–0.75 inch margins.
        
    - Subtle horizontal lines to separate sections.
        
    - Bold job titles and italics for companies/dates.
        
    - Use minimal colors: dark gray, soft blue, or navy accent.
