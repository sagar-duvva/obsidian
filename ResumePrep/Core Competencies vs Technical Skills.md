Absolutely! Let’s break down **Core Competencies** vs **Technical Skills** clearly:

---

### **1. Core Competencies**

**Definition:**  
These are the fundamental abilities, traits, or behaviors that enable a person to perform effectively across different jobs or roles. They are more **soft skills or personal attributes** that are transferable across multiple contexts.

**Characteristics:**

- Broad and generalizable across roles and industries.
    
- Often linked to leadership, communication, problem-solving, and collaboration.
    
- Focus on _how_ you approach work rather than _what_ you specifically know.
    

**Examples:**

- Communication
    
- Teamwork / Collaboration
    
- Problem-solving
    
- Adaptability
    
- Leadership
    
- Critical thinking
    

**Use Case:**  
Employers look for these to assess whether a candidate can **fit into the organizational culture** and thrive in diverse situations.

---

### **2. Technical Skills**

**Definition:**  
These are **specific, teachable abilities or knowledge** required to perform particular tasks. They are often **hard skills** that can be measured or tested.

**Characteristics:**

- Specific to a role, industry, or technology.
    
- Can be learned through training, education, or hands-on experience.
    
- Focus on _what_ you know and _how_ you apply it to a task.
    

**Examples:**

- Programming languages (Python, Java)
    
- Data analysis (Excel, SQL, Tableau)
    
- Engineering or machinery operation
    
- Graphic design (Photoshop, Illustrator)
    
- Accounting software (QuickBooks, SAP)
    

**Use Case:**  
Employers check these to determine if you have the **practical ability to perform the job’s technical requirements**.

---

### **Key Differences at a Glance**

|Feature|Core Competencies|Technical Skills|
|---|---|---|
|Nature|Soft skills / behavioral|Hard skills / task-specific knowledge|
|Transferability|Across roles/industries|Usually role-specific|
|Measurability|Qualitative, harder to measure|Quantitative, easier to test|
|Focus|How you work and interact|What you can do|
|Examples|Leadership, communication, adaptability|Excel, Python, CAD software|

---

💡 **Quick way to remember:**

- **Core Competencies** = _how you do your work_
    
- **Technical Skills** = _what you know to do your work_
